Minerals and Energy Resources

Complete Class 10 Geography Notes

I. What is a Mineral & Mode of Occurrence

Geologists define a mineral as a "homogenous, naturally occurring substance with a definable internal structure." Minerals are usually found in ores[cite: 3].

Minerals generally occur in veins and lodes, sedimentary beds/layers, weathering residual masses, alluvial placer deposits, and ocean waters[cite: 3].

II. Classification of Minerals

Mineral Type Description & Examples
Metallic (Ferrous) Contain iron; provide a base for metallurgical industries. Examples: Iron ore, Manganese, Nickel[cite: 3].
Metallic (Non-Ferrous) Do not contain iron. Examples: Copper, Bauxite, Lead, Zinc, Gold[cite: 3].
Non-Metallic Examples: Mica, Salt, Potash, Limestone, Granite, Marble[cite: 3].

III. Energy Resources

  • Conventional Sources: Coal, petroleum, natural gas, and electricity (hydel and thermal)[cite: 3].
  • Non-Conventional Sources: Solar, wind, tidal, geothermal, biogas, and atomic energy[cite: 3].
