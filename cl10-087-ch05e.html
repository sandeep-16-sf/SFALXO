Chapter 5: Consumer Rights

Class 10 Economics Notes

I. The Consumer in the Marketplace & Consumer Movement

Consumer Rights: Legal protections and entitlements safeguarding consumers against unfair trade practices, adulteration, exploitation, and deficient services[cite: 8].
  • Marketplace Exploitation: Consumers often face weak bargaining positions, under-weighing, adulterated goods, hidden charges, and false advertising by powerful corporations[cite: 8].
  • Consumer Movement: Born in the 1960s out of public dissatisfaction with food shortages, hoarding, and black marketing, leading to the enactment of COPRA in 1986 (amended in 2019)[cite: 8].

II. Consumer Rights & Quality Certification

Core Rights & Standards Overview
Key Consumer Rights Right to safety, right to be informed (MRP, ingredients), right to choose, right to seek redressal, right to representation, and consumer education[cite: 8].
Quality Certification Marks Standardization logos like ISI, Agmark, Hallmark, and +F assure product quality, making certification mandatory for mass consumption and safety items[cite: 8].

III. Redressal Machinery

Under COPRA, a three-tier quasi-judicial system was established: District Commissions (claims up to Rs 1 crore), State Commissions (Rs 1 crore to Rs 10 crore), and the National Commission (exceeding Rs 10 crore) to provide accessible justice and compensation to aggrieved consumers[cite: 8].
