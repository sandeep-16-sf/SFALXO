I. Importance and Classification of Industries

Production of goods in large quantities after processing from raw materials to more valuable products is called manufacturing[cite: 2]. Manufacturing sector is considered the backbone of economic development[cite: 2].

Industries are classified based on raw materials (agro-based, mineral-based), main role (basic, consumer), capital investment (small scale), ownership (public, private, joint, cooperative), and bulk/weight (heavy, light)[cite: 2].

II. Major Industries in India

Industry Key Details & Locations
Textile Industry Unique position; self-reliant and complete in the value chain. First successful mill in Mumbai (1854)[cite: 2].
Iron and Steel Industry Basic industry; Chhotanagpur plateau region has maximum concentration due to relative advantages[cite: 2].
Chemical & Fertilizer Fast growing; includes inorganic and organic sectors (petrochemicals). Fertilizers focus on nitrogenous (urea) and phosphatic fertilizers[cite: 2].

III. Industrial Pollution & Environmental Degradation

Industries cause four types of pollution: Air, Water, Land, and Noise[cite: 2]. Sustainable development requires minimizing waste generation, treating effluents before release, fitting smoke stacks with electrostatic precipitators, and planting green belts[cite: 2].
